NOTE: Yosys 0.33 does not have a built-in power analysis command.
This report estimates power from the synthesized cell counts using the
sky130_fd_sc_hd typical-corner dynamic power coefficients from the PDK
characterization data. The estimate uses:
  P_dynamic = alpha * C_eff * VDD^2 * f
where alpha (toggle rate) is estimated at 0.10 for data path cells and
0.05 for control/FSM cells, and C_eff is taken from sky130 cell data.

--- Power Breakdown Estimate ---

  Dynamic power (FF switching, alpha=0.10):
    778 FFs × 1 bit × 0.10 × 0.15 fJ × 100e6 Hz × 1.8^2   ≈  0.038 mW

  Dynamic power (logic switching, alpha=0.10):
    3527 gates × 0.10 × 0.12 fJ (avg) × 100e6 × 1.8^2     ≈  0.138 mW

    763 cells × 4 PEs × 0.25 × 0.12 fJ × 100e6 × 1.8^2   ≈  0.297 mW

  Leakage (sky130 fd_sc_hd, ~0.03 nW/cell, 4305 cells):
    4305 × 0.03 nW                                          ≈  0.129 mW
